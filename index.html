1 Fill Your Details

Start from the sample details and make them yours — anything optional has an on/off toggle, and all 16 designs update live.

Images are session-only: cropped photos and logos apply instantly across every design, and everything returns to the default look after a refresh. Logos also lend their colours to the palette automatically — fine-tune it any time in the Colour scheme group.

2 Browse & Copy

One unified system — every design adapts to the same details, images and colour scheme, so each works for professional, academic, corporate or personal mail alike. Pick the silhouette you like, then copy it with icons and links intact, or take the tidy text version.

3 Paste Into Your Mail Client

Use Copy Signature for a one-shot paste — icons, images and their links stay intact. Prefer plain text? The Text version button on each card carries the same details with simple links.

Gmail

  1. Click Copy Signature on your favourite card.
  2. Open Gmail → ⚙ Settings → See all settings → General → Signature.
  3. Click Create new, name it, then Ctrl/Cmd + V into the box.
  4. Scroll down → Save Changes. Done.

Outlook

  1. Click Copy Signature on your favourite card.
  2. Open Outlook → Settings → Mail → Compose and reply.
  3. In Email signature, click New signature, name it, then Ctrl/Cmd + V.
  4. Save, then select it under Signature for new messages.

Apple Mail & Mobile

  1. Apple Mail: Mail → Settings → Signatures → + → paste into the right pane.
  2. Gmail app: paste into the signature field via the Gmail web app first, then it syncs.
  3. Mobile: signatures over ~4 lines get trimmed visually — try The One-Liner or Compact Footer.

Pro Tips

  • Paste with Ctrl/Cmd + V — the icons and their links travel with it.
  • Put professional links first (portfolio, LinkedIn) — reorder them in Step 1.
  • Crop photos to a tidy rectangle before adding — square-ish frames look best.
  • Grab the Text version for mail apps that prefer plain text.
  • Send yourself a test email to confirm everything looks right.
